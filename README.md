# Chocolate Shipment Data Analysis ( Interactive Dashboard using PowerBI )
## Project Objective
The goal of this project is to develop a comprehensive shipment analytics dashboard that transforms raw business data into actionable insights. It empowers stakeholders to monitor product performance, track regional sales, and evaluate team profitability for better decision‑making.

## Dataset Used
- <a href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/11limPgGTRuPMvTktIpuPGC9MKuy1UdfK/edit?usp=sharingouid=109902785254339016824rtpof=truesd=true">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs)
- What is the total shipment amount generated during the selected period?
- What is the total profit generated from all shipments?
- What is the total number of boxes shipped?
- Which product has the highest total sales amount?
- Which products contribute the most to overall sales?
- Which products generate the highest profit?
- Which geographical region/country contributes the highest sales amount?
- How is the total sales amount distributed across different countries?
- What is the profit percentage achieved by each sales team?
- Which sales team has the highest profit percentage?
- How are shipments distributed across different shipment amounts?
- Who are the top salespeople based on total sales amount?
- Which salesperson has the highest number of boxes sold?
- Which salesperson has the highest profit percentage?
- Which products have relatively low or negative profit?
- What are the top 6 products based on their sales performance?
- How do total sales and profit compare across different countries?
